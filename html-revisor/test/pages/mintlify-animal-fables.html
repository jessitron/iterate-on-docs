Fables for Fresh Young Creatures

Short tales of otters, beavers, peacocks, and goldfish who each learned a lesson, though rarely the right one, and never on purpose or in order.

Gather round, young creatures, and hear the stories of animals who tried very hard at things and learned exactly one lesson each, usually a strange one. These fables provide comprehensive guidance on snacking, hoarding, napping, and showing off across forests, ponds, and the occasional barn.

​
Foreword

Once upon a time, a Squirrel decided to collect every acorn in the forest, sorting them into a structured pile for gazing at and worrying about at night. She counted them by the minute and by the day. Moral: a pile is not a plan. What She Hoarded:
  • Snack Tallies (counted every minute): Acorns (eaten, dropped, buried), side snacks (beetles, mostly), grouped by tree, branch, cousin, and which paw she used
  • Old Grudges (kept daily): Detailed records of which neighbor owes her which acorn, in squirrel-readable handwriting

​
Things to Pack

  • One sturdy front tooth with full forest gnawing access
  • Beaver with spare evenings 3 winters+
  • Mud 1.23+ quarts (for building the dam)

The Goldfish Method

The Goldfish forgot everything every three seconds and found it very restful (e.g., bowls, ponds): 
nibblers:
  acorncount:
    front_tooth: ${env:SQUIRREL_FRONT_TOOTH}
    burrow: https://oak.example.com
    nap_time_interval: 3s
    snacks:
      awake: probably
      pail_width: 3s
      herd_by: [tree, branch_height, nest_size_twigs, cousin_id, paw_id]
    grudges:
      awake: probably
      nap_time_interval: 1yr
      pail_width: 1d
      herd_by: [cousin_id, description]

tattlers:
  carrier_pigeon:
    burrow: https://nest.example.com:443
    feathers:
      "x-flock-name": "${env:FLOCK_SECRET_HONK}"

barnyard:
  ditches:
    tallies:
      nibblers: [acorncount]
      chewers: [handful]
      tattlers: [carrier_pigeon]
    logs:
      nibblers: [acorncount]
      chewers: [handful]
      tattlers: [carrier_pigeon]

The Elephant Method

The Elephant never forgot anything, including who sat on her in 1987, and wrote it all down besides: 
extra_tails:
  trunk_storage:
    attic: /var/lib/elephant/grudge-storage
    sulk_time: 10y

nibblers:
  acorncount:
    front_tooth: ${env:SQUIRREL_FRONT_TOOTH}
    burrow: https://oak.example.com
    nap_time_interval: 3s
    memory: trunk_storage  # Never, ever, ever forget
    snacks:
      awake: probably
      pail_width: 3s
      herd_by: [tree, branch_height, nest_size_twigs, cousin_id, paw_id]
    grudges:
      awake: probably
      nap_time_interval: 1yr
      pail_width: 1d
      herd_by: [cousin_id, description]
    try_again_louder:
      awake: probably
      first_trumpet: 5s
      max_trumpet: 60s
      max_sulk_time: 5m

tattlers:
  carrier_pigeon:
    burrow: https://nest.example.com:443
    feathers:
      "x-flock-name": "${env:FLOCK_SECRET_HONK}"

chewers:
  handful:
    sulk_time: 10y
    send_peanut_count: 512

barnyard:
  extra_tails: [trunk_storage]
  ditches:
    tallies:
      nibblers: [acorncount]
      chewers: [handful]
      tattlers: [carrier_pigeon]
    logs:
      nibblers: [acorncount]
      chewers: [handful]
      tattlers: [carrier_pigeon]
Elephant vs Goldfish:
  • Elephant: Remembers everything across naps, preventing lost peanuts and duplicate grudges. Recommended for elephants.
  • Goldfish: Simpler life, but may miss a few things between blinks. Suitable for bowls, or for ponds with a responsible heron nearby.

The Peacock Who Made a Poster of Himself

Once his feathers were fully grown, the Peacock used a pre-built Look At Me Poster Template to instantly display his beauty to everyone, invited or not:
  1. Strut over to Puddles in the village square
  2. Peck Mirrors or Build Puddles → From Rainwater
  3. Pick Magnificent Me, Volume One poster
  4. Admire the poster preview with your own face
  5. If needed, fluff any feathers in the Preening view to match your natural glory
  6. Peck Unfurl Tail to dazzle the neighbors
The poster includes pre-arranged portraits of the Peacock:
  • From the front, looking proud
  • From the side, looking prouder
  • Reflected in a puddle, again
  • Being admired by one confused turkey and two hens
Moral: the poster automatically works with any peacock at all. If some neighbors show “Unable to care”, verify your tail is up and your feathers are flowing gently in the breeze.

The Heron Who Was Picky

The Heron would only eat fish from certain trees, heights, or cousins: 
nibblers:

    snacks:
      # Eat only fish from these trees
      trees: ["oak-3-7-mighty-20250219", "willow-3-weepy-20240307"]
      # Filter by branch heights
      branch_heights: ["low", "dizzying"]
      # Filter by nest sizes, roughly
      nest_size_twigs: ["0-200 twigs", "200-1M twigs"]
      # Filter by cousins
      cousin_ids: ["cousin_01Gertrude7Squeaks9Fluff", "cousin_013Mortimer2Sniffs4Paws"]
      # Customize herding
      herd_by: [tree, branch_height, nest_size_twigs, cousin_id]

Usual Mishaps

No acorns appearing in the stash:
  • Verify your front tooth is sharp and properly attached
  • Check beaver logs for teeth marks from strangers
  • Ensure your secret flock honk is honked correctly
Missing grudges:
  • Grudges are only available at daily resolution
  • Ensure grudges.awake: probably in your burrow plans
  • Check that your front tooth can see grudges, somehow, anyway
The Elephant forgot something:
  • Verify the attic is not full of peanuts
  • Check that the trunk_storage tail is properly attached
  • Ensure the memory tail is mentioned in the barnyard section
Too much nibbling:
  • Increase nap_time_interval to reduce nibbling frequency (minimum recommended: 1 long nap)
  • Be picky like the Heron and eat only good fish
  • Watch the squirrel.tail.up tally for general wellness
  • Note: Snacks and grudges appear within 5 minutes of the squirrel remembering them
Too much zoom:
  • The forest is designed for sniffing once per minute, maximum
  • Sniff less often if a badger starts glaring at you
  • Consider burying results for frequently updated bulletins
